    Frequently asked questions

    Didn’t find your question? Ask it on Telegram and I’ll reply within a day.

    I have Instagram. Why do I need a website too?

    Instagram is seen by people who already follow you. New clients search on Google: “cleaning Warsaw”, “barber near me”, and the site brings exactly them to you. Instagram stays and is linked to the site.

    I’m not good with tech. Will I manage?

    Yes, there’s nothing for you to set up. From you: a 5–15 minute brief and a 20-minute call. I do the rest and explain everything in plain words.

    What is a brief, and do I have to fill it in?

    It’s a questionnaire about your business and how you see your site. Only your name and contact are required, the rest is optional. The more detail you give, the more precisely I can name the price and timeline.

    How does payment work? Will there be a contract?

    Yes, I work under a contract and fix the price before work starts. A 50% prepayment, after which I prepare three prototypes for you to choose from. The rest after launch.

    How many edits are included in the price?

    Edits to the chosen version are included: you tell me what to change, and I make the changes until you’re happy. New pages and features after launch we agree on separately.

    How long until launch?

    I show the first version in 3–7 days, and launch is usually 1–2 weeks from the start.

    How much does the site cost after launch?

    What remains is the domain and hosting. With support from 150 zł a month, they’re already included, along with edits and updates.

    What if you disappear?

    The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and I hand over all access after launch. The code is clean, so any developer can work with it.

    Why not build a site on Wix myself?

    You can, but it takes your time: learning the website builder, writing the texts, setting up booking and Google. A template is hard to fit to your business, and the site stays on the builder’s platform. With me, the domain is registered in your name.

    What about GDPR and cookies?

    The site doesn’t collect unnecessary data. If it has an enquiry form or analytics, I add a privacy policy and a cookie banner.
